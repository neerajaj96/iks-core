Scope and evidence model

The graph covers the top-level IKS taxonomy, 64 Kāmasūtra aṅga-vidyā items, all 3,983 Aṣṭādhyāyī sūtras, 37 Vyākaraṇa terms, and 2,228 Dhātus. Every historical assertion is reified as an iks:Claim with a source citation, locator, verification status, and method.

Verification statuses are verified_primary, verified_secondary, flagged_uncertain, and editorial. Records marked unverified_hypothesis are never exported.
