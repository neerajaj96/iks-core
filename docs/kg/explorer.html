About this explorer

The explorer is a static view over the published IKS Knowledge Graph. It reads the same Turtle graph as the downloadable release; no separate or hand-edited records are served.

What you can inspect

  • 2,228 Dhātupāṭha roots with gaṇa, pada, Sanskrit, IAST, SLP1, and English meanings.
  • 3,983 Aṣṭādhyāyī sūtras with number, topic note, and three transliterations.
  • 37 Vyākaraṇa terms with definitions and their defining sūtras.
  • Every reified claim, citation locator, evidence span or record hash, and verification status.

Verification

verified_primary is direct primary-source evidence. verified_secondary is an independent dataset cross-check. flagged_uncertain marks a documented uncertainty.

The graph has overall, and claims are indexed by this explorer's three entity views. The remainder concern taxonomy and text metadata.

Data and rights

The graph and explorer data are generated from pinned source snapshots. Source texts retain their own licenses, including CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, educational-use, and reference-use terms; the repository's MIT license does not override them.

Reproducibility

Explorer JSON is built by kg/tools/build_explorer.py from docs/kg/iks-kg.ttl.gz. CI runs the builder in --check mode, verifies artifact hashes, and validates the graph against SHACL.
